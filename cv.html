Research Profile

PhD researcher focusing on agricultural systems, ecological sustainability, and planetary boundaries. My research quantifies trade-embodied ecological pressures, with particular emphasis on ecosystem loss, land degradation, climate stress, and ecological equity. I combine multi-regional input–output modelling, spatial analysis, machine learning, and scenario modelling to investigate the links between consumption, trade, and ecological limits.

Research Experience

PhD Researcher Sep 2022 – Present
Northwest A&F University, China

Trade-Embodied Ecosystem Accounting

  • Developed consumption-based accounting frameworks to quantify belowground ecosystem multifunctionality (BEMF) embodied in agricultural trade.
  • Integrated spatial soil-function data with agricultural production and trade data to trace ecosystem pressures from production regions to final consumption across 192 regions and 123 sectors.
  • Quantified ecological pressures associated with China's agricultural imports and evaluated their distribution across exporting regions under climate change.

Deep Learning for Land-Use Scenario Synthesis

  • Developed generative deep-learning models, including Variational Autoencoder (VAE), Conditional GAN (CGAN), and Recurrent Conditional GAN (RCGAN), to synthesize global land-use trajectories from CMIP6/LUH2 scenarios.
  • Generated 30,000 physically plausible land-use pathways and developed a computationally efficient approach for exploring large scenario spaces.
  • Combined Random Forest and SHAP analysis to identify major drivers of land degradation risk under SDG 15.3.1.

Provincial Ecological Efficiency and Spillover Networks

  • Evaluated the efficiency of China's 31 provinces in transforming agricultural innovation into land ecological security using Slacks-Based Measure (SBM) efficiency models with undesirable outputs.
  • Constructed interprovincial spillover networks using a modified gravity model and applied CONCOR block modelling and MRQAP to investigate structural and functional patterns of ecological spillovers.
  • Identified factors associated with interprovincial differences and spillover relationships in agricultural ecological security.
